    Our Story

    A Legacy of Authentic Indian Flavours

    It began in 1955 in a small kitchen in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Our founder, with a passion for preserving the bold flavours of traditional Indian cuisine, started crafting pickles using age-old family recipes.

    Seventy years later, Pickle Gallery — or Mittal Achar as we're known across Indore — continues that same mission. Every jar follows the same recipes, the same natural ingredients, the same process that made us beloved generations ago.

    We believe food is memory. A jar of our mango pickle can transport you to your grandmother's kitchen. That's the magic we chase with every batch we make.

    Today we ship pan-India, sell on Amazon, and serve customers from Kashmir to Kanyakumari. But our heart remains in Vijay Nagar, Indore — where it all began.
